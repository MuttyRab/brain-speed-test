Brain Speed Test
A science-backed battery of 5 validated cognitive tests measuring how fast your brain processes information — compared to population norms.
⚡
Simple Reaction
Raw neural speed. How fast you detect & respond to a stimulus.
🎯
Choice Reaction
Decision speed. Pick the right target among distractors.
🎨
Stroop Inhibition
Cognitive control. Override automatic reading response.
🧩
Pattern Memory
Working memory speed. Recall and reproduce sequences.
🛑
Go / No-Go
Inhibitory control. Respond to targets, suppress responses to non-targets.
Grounded in peer-reviewed research. Benchmarks derived from MindCrowd (159,000+ participants), UK Biobank (500,000+ subjects), Deary-Liewald reaction time paradigm, Stroop (1935) interference effect, and NIH Toolbox Cognition Battery norms. Results are compared to healthy adult averages.
